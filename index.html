Top 3 target companies
Host training establishments

I compared three companies where I would like to do my Work Immersion, using the same criteria for each: company profile, industry type, reputation, learning opportunities, accessibility, and career growth potential.

Option 1

Webcast Technologies

Information technology · Quezon City

A private Philippine IT company founded in 2000. It offers GIS, GPS tracking systems, software development and mobile application development, with branches in Batangas, Cagayan de Oro, Davao and Cebu.

Advantages: hands-on work in mapping, tracking and mobile apps; a smaller team where interns can be noticed.

Disadvantages: less well known, and the head office is far from Muntinlupa City.

Option 2

Globe Telecom

Telecommunications · Bonifacio Global City

A publicly listed telecommunications company, founded in 1935 as Globe Wireless and known as Globe Telecom since 1992. It is described as the largest mobile network operator in the Philippines, and its GCash payment service is a subsidiary.

Advantages: a strong reputation, large digital products used by millions of people, and published internship programs.

Disadvantages: a big company means more competition for places, and the commute to Taguig takes time.

Option 3

Azeus Systems Philippines

Software and IT services · Manila

The Manila systems development centre of Azeus, set up in 1994, with a second development centre in Cebu. The centres are appraised at CMMI Level 5, and Azeus sells the Azeus Convene paperless meeting application.

Advantages: mature software processes, a real product with real users, and posted internships.

Disadvantages: more focused on development than on design, and the exact office location still needs to be confirmed.

Reflection

How has preparing for Work Immersion helped me understand my career goals and future professional responsibilities?

Preparing for Work Immersion has shown me that my goal is not just to finish internship hours. It is to grow as a designer. Before this, I thought about my future in very general terms. Researching Webcast Technologies, Globe Telecom and Azeus Systems Philippines made me compare real companies by the work they do, the learning they offer, how far they are, and where a career could go. I learned that the best choice is not always the biggest name. It is the place where I can practice my design skills on real work and learn from experienced people.

Building this portfolio also helped me see my own strengths. As the UI/UX designer of our capstone project, SkillConnect, I created wireframes, a clickable prototype and a design system in Figma for three kinds of users: clients, workers and admins. Putting that work in one place showed me how much I have already done, and what I still need to improve, such as explaining my design decisions clearly and backing them with feedback from real users. My Java Fundamentals course and the Cyber Security event I joined remind me that good design also needs an understanding of how systems are built and kept safe.

I also understand that a professional has responsibilities beyond skill. I must be on time, communicate clearly, work well with a team, accept feedback, and take care of the quality of what I submit. Work Immersion is my first chance to practice these habits in a real workplace. My career objective is to maximize my design skills, and this preparation gave me a clear plan: learn from my HTE, ask questions, apply feedback, and keep building a portfolio that tells my professional story.
